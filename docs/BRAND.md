# How the survey follows the brand principles (v6, August 2026)

The survey is a product surface, so it follows the "paper" rules, not the film and social rules.

| Principle | What the survey does |
|-----------|----------------------|
| The product stands on Paper | Page ground is Paper `#FAF7F1`. Questions sit on white cards with a soft shadow, never a hard border. No dark mode: the product never goes dark. |
| One blue, quietly | Every word, border, progress bar and the Send button is Royal Blue `#00198B`. Softer text uses Royal at 75, 56 and 40 percent. There is no second colour: no green, no red, no purple, no gold. Error copy is plain royal text, a note rather than an alarm. |
| Chip tint | Unselected chips and the rating squares use Chip `#F5EFE4`. Selection is carried by weight and shape: a royal border and heavier text on white, not a colour change. |
| Sentence case, always | No block capitals anywhere, including the brand label, button text and the mono signal labels, which are lowercase. |
| Type | Inter for every title and sentence (700 titles with tight tracking, 400 copy at 1.62 leading, 500 labels). Spline Sans Mono, tracked 0.06em, only for the signal layer: the "jeani · feedback" label, hints such as "pick all that apply", and the scale anchors. Never body copy. |
| Stillness is the feeling | One idea per card, wide spacing, nothing flashing. No emoji, no exclamation marks. |
| Report, never urge | Copy notices rather than cheers: "How's it going so far?", "Thank you. Every one of these is read by the people building Jeani." Nothing congratulates. |
| Your normal, never the average | A design option reads "It compares me to my own normal". No population comparisons appear anywhere. |
| Plain words only | Options use everyday words: "Sleep and rest", "Moving well for life", "Not enough time with it yet". Never performance, crush, grind, optimise, elite. |
| The goal frame | Question 2 asks for the goal the person set, in the brand's own list: a marathon, a comeback, moving well for life. |

## Email block

Email clients cannot load the shadow or the web fonts reliably, so the block is a white card on a Paper background with Inter requested and the system sans-serif as fallback. The five buttons are Chip tint with royal numerals, none of them highlighted: the person chooses, the design does not lead. Anchors are lowercase mono: "not for me" and "working well".

## When editing copy

Run each sentence past the ten rules on page 16 of the deck: report not urge, insight before feature, one idea per surface, your normal not the average, show the reason, name the source, years not sessions, plain words, no medical claims, sentence case.
